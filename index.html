Flexible Schedules at Work

Last year, a mid-sized company noticed that many employees were arriving late because of heavy traffic during the morning rush hour. Instead of changing the official start time for everyone, management introduced a flexible schedule. Employees could begin work any time between 7:00 and 9:00 a.m., as long as they completed their required hours and attended scheduled meetings.

At first, some managers were concerned that the new system might make teamwork more difficult. However, after three months, attendance improved and employees reported lower stress levels. The company also found that most teams continued to meet deadlines and communicate effectively. Because of these results, management decided to keep the flexible schedule and review it again at the end of the year.
